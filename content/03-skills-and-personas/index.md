# Unit 3 — Skills & Personas

Access is not competence. An agent with your files and calendar still needs to know the method, standards, and limits of the work it is helping with. This unit is about capturing that know-how in a form that can be inspected and improved.

## Follow the idea

[[skills-teach-ai-how|Skills Teach AI How]] turns a repeated procedure into something an agent can follow, test, and improve. [[personas-a-specialist-you-shaped|Personas: A Specialist You Shaped]] adds the perspective and standards that help the same system approach a research question, a business problem, or a creative brief differently.

Together, they move the instruction from “be helpful” to a visible working method: what to gather, how to proceed, what good looks like, and when to stop for human input.
