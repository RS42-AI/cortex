# Unit 5 — Leverage in Practice

The system is not the destination. The point is what it allows a person to attempt: running a small business with better support, exploring an opportunity faster, studying a difficult subject, or building a creative tool that can listen, analyze, and help shape new music.

## Follow the idea

[[05-leverage-in-practice/one-person-working-business|One Person, A Working Business]] shows how connected agents can give one person the operating capacity of a larger team. [[05-leverage-in-practice/what-the-human-still-owns|What the Human Still Owns]] draws the boundary around the judgment, taste, responsibility, and direction that make the leverage worth having.

These are examples, not limits. The same building blocks—memory, connections, skills, and bounded autonomy—can support work in business, science, education, music, or everyday life. The useful question is: what becomes possible when the routine parts stop consuming all of your attention?
